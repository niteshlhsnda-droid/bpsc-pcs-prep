The exam at a glance — 71st CCE pattern

StageFormatMarks
Prelims150 MCQs, General Studies (2 hrs)150 — screening only
MainsHindi (qualifying) + GS-I + GS-II + Optional + Essay (3 hrs each)1050 in merit
InterviewPersonality test120

Final merit = 1170 (Mains 1050 + Interview 120). Negative marking: ⅓ mark per wrong answer in Prelims only. Medium: Hindi or English — both fully accepted.

This pattern follows the 71st CCE notification (latest verified, September 2026). BPSC changed the mains pattern between the 70th and 71st CCE — always re-check the official notification at the BPSC website before you plan.

# One-Attempt Plan — 12-Month Timetable

This plan assumes ~6–8 focused hours/day. Scale the hours, not the sequence. The non-negotiable
principle: **Prelims and Mains are prepared together** — 70% of the syllabus overlaps, and
Mains answer-writing is what converts knowledge into marks.

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Months 1–4)

**Goal:** finish the entire syllabus once, with notes in your own words.

- **Daily split:** 4 hrs core subject + 1.5 hrs Bihar portion + 1 hr current affairs + 30 min
  revision of yesterday.
- **Subject order:** Polity → Modern History → Geography → Economy → Environment → Science →
  Ancient/Medieval History.
- **Bihar parallel track:** one Bihar notes file per week (`notes/bihar-*.md` here are your
  starters — expand them).
- **Output:** handwritten/typed one-page summaries per chapter. If you can't summarize it in one
  page, you don't know it.
- **PYQs:** 1 prelims paper/week, untimed, open-book (`pyq/strategy.md`).
- **End of Phase 1 test:** you should be able to explain any syllabus topic aloud for 2 minutes.

## Phase 2 — Consolidation (Months 5–8)

**Goal:** second reading + answer writing begins + Bihar becomes your strength.

- **Daily split:** 3 hrs revision of Phase-1 notes + 2 hrs answer writing + 1.5 hrs CA + 1 hr
  Bihar rapid-fire.
- **Answer writing (start month 5):** 2 GS answers/day → 4/day by month 8. Use the
  intro–body–conclusion structure; get them evaluated (peer group or mentor).
- **Essay:** 1 essay/week from month 6, rotating buckets (social, Bihar, economy, governance,
  tech, philosophical).
- **PYQs:** 1 timed prelims paper/week + theme tagging; start your Mains answer bank.
- **Optional subject:** 300 marks — give it 1.5 hrs/day throughout Phase 2 if not finished.

## Phase 3 — Prelims sprint (Months 9–10)

**Goal:** prelims-ready, without abandoning Mains muscles.

- **Daily split:** 3 hrs mock + analysis + 2 hrs weak-area repair + 1.5 hrs CA revision +
  1 hr answer writing (maintain the habit — don't let it die).
- **Mocks:** 2 full prelims mocks/week under strict timing (2 hrs, OMR-style discipline).
- **The ⅓ rule:** track blind-guess vs educated-guess accuracy separately; finalize your
  attempt strategy per subject.
- **Bihar + CA:** these two sections decide prelims. Your rapid-fire file should be at
  recall-in-seconds level.

## Phase 4 — Mains & interview (Months 11–12, post-prelims)

**Goal:** convert.

- **Mains:** full 3-hour papers weekly per GS paper; essay every week; Hindi qualifying practice
  (don't fail the 30% — one hour/week is enough).
- **Revision:** only your own one-page summaries + error log + repeat-theme list. No new books.
- **Interview (after mains):** DAF-based preparation — know your district, your graduation
  subject, Bihar's current affairs, and your hobbies deeply. Mock interviews ×3 minimum.

## Weekly rhythm (all phases)

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Mon–Sat | As per phase split above |
| Sunday | Full revision day: no new topics. Weekly mock + error log + rapid-fire drill |

## Rules that make "one attempt" real

1. **Single sources.** One book per subject (see `booklist.md`). New material in month 10 is
   sabotage.
2. **Error log.** Every mock mistake goes in a notebook with the *reason* (concept gap / silly
   error / guess). Review it before every mock.
3. **No zero days.** Even on bad days: 1 hour of rapid-fire revision counts.
4. **Health is syllabus.** Sleep 7 hours; walk daily. Burnout fails more attempts than the
   paper does.
5. **Cut the noise.** One newspaper, one CA magazine, this repo. Unfollow everything else.

Prelims vs Mains — One Preparation, Two Skills

The biggest planning mistake in BPSC prep is treating Prelims and Mains as two separate exams. They are one syllabus tested with two different skills. Prepare once, revise twice.

Head-to-head

PrelimsMains
Format150 MCQs, 2 hoursDescriptive papers, 3 hours each
Marks150 (qualifying only)1050 + 120 interview = merit
Negative marking⅓ per wrong answerNone
Pace~48 seconds per question8–10 min per answer
TestsRecognition of factsAnalysis, structure, expression
Bihar weight~20–25%~30–40% of GS-II alone

The overlap — study these once, use them twice

  • Same NCERT base for history, geography, polity, economy, science.
  • Same current affairs — Prelims asks the fact, Mains asks the analysis of the same event.
  • Same Bihar facts — rivers, schemes, firsts, personalities appear in both.
  • Same PYQ themes — a Prelims MCQ theme this year is a Mains question next year.

What needs separate preparation

Prelims-only skills: elimination technique, 48-second pacing, educated guessing under negative marking, rapid-fire factual recall. Train with timed mocks — see the Prelims blueprint.

Mains-only skills: the intro–body–conclusion discipline, hand-drawn diagrams (flowcharts, maps, timelines), data + article + scheme value-addition, Bihar examples in every GS-II answer, 3-hour writing stamina. Train with daily timed answer writing — see what toppers do.

The integrated weekly rhythm

PhasePrelims shareMains share
Months 1–830% (facts, MCQs)70% (notes, answer writing)
Months 9–1050 / 5050 / 50
Last 60 days before Prelims90%10% (maintain writing hand)
Prelims → Mains gap10%90%

The rule: learn the fact first (Prelims depth), then learn to argue it (Mains depth). A topic isn't "done" until you can both tick its MCQ and write 200 words on it with a diagram.

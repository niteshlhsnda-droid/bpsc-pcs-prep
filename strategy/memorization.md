# Memorization System — How to Retain Everything

BPSC rewards **recall speed**, not reading speed. This system is built on three proven
principles: active recall, spaced repetition, and elaboration (connecting facts to stories).

## 1. Active recall — close the book

- After reading any topic, **close the book and write down everything you remember**. The
  struggle to retrieve is what builds the memory — re-reading feels productive and isn't.
- Convert every notes page into **questions**, not statements. "When was Champaran?" beats
  "Champaran was 1917" because the exam asks questions.
- **Blank-page test:** weekly, take a blank page and dump everything you know about one
  subject. Gaps you discover are your next week's targets.

## 2. Spaced repetition — the revision calendar

Memory decays on a curve; each review resets it. Use fixed intervals:

| Review | When |
|---|---|
| 1st | Same evening (30 min before sleep) |
| 2nd | Next day morning |
| 3rd | After 7 days (Sunday revision) |
| 4th | After 30 days |
| 5th | After 90 days |

- Maintain a simple **revision tracker** (notebook or spreadsheet): topic × dates reviewed.
- The rapid-fire file (`bihar-gk-rapid-fire.md`) is designed for reviews 2–5 — one-liners are
  spaced-repetition fuel.

## 3. Mnemonics — Bihar edition

Build your own; personal mnemonics stick better than borrowed ones. Starters:

- **Bihar's 8 famous rivers:** "Ga-Ga-Ko-So-Ba-Ma-Pu-Fa" → **Ganga, Gandak, Kosi, Son, Bagmati,
  Mahananda, Punpun, Falgu.**
- **Ancient universities:** "Na-Vi" → **Na**landa (Gupta/Kumaragupta) → **Vi**kramshila (Pala/
  Dharmapala). Alphabetical order = chronological order.
- **Freedom timeline in Bihar:** 1857 Kunwar Singh → 1917 Champaran → 1942 Quit India →
  1974 JP Movement. Anchor: *57–17–42–74*.
- **GI agri products:** "Li-Za-Ka-Pa-Ma-Ma" → **L**itchi (Shahi), **Za**rdalu, **Ka**tarni,
  **Pa**an (Magahi), **Ma**khana, **Ma**richa rice.
- **State symbols:** "S-G-K-P-M" → **S**parrow (bird), **G**aur (animal), **K**achnar (flower),
  **P**eepal (tree), **M**ango (fruit).

## 4. Map work — geography you can draw

- **Draw Bihar's outline from memory weekly**: mark the Ganga, 8 rivers, Patna, Gaya,
  Bhagalpur, Muzaffarpur, Darbhanga, Purnia, Valmiki TR, Nepal border.
- Drawing beats looking. In the exam hall you won't have the map — but you'll have the
  mental image you drew 20 times.

## 5. Story-linking — history you can't forget

- Don't memorize "Sher Shah Suri — Sasaram — rupiya — Grand Trunk Road" as four facts.
  Tell it as one story: *a boy from Sasaram builds an empire's administration so good the
  Mughals copy it.*
- Champaran: *a farmer (Raj Kumar Shukla) travels to Lucknow to fetch a lawyer (Gandhi) —
  and changes Indian history.* Stories survive; lists don't.

## 6. The daily revision system (30 minutes, non-negotiable)

1. **10 min** — rapid-fire file, aloud, timed. Mark the ones you fumble.
2. **10 min** — yesterday's one-page summaries, blank-page style.
3. **10 min** — error log: re-attempt 5 old mistakes.

## 7. What to do when you forget (you will)

Forgetting is the system working — it tells you what needs another repetition. Never
re-read the whole chapter; **re-test the specific gap**, then move its next review closer.
Trust the calendar, not your anxiety.

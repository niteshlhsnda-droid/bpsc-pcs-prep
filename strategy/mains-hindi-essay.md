# Mains: General Hindi + Essay

Two papers candidates chronically under-prepare — and both are where attempts quietly die.
Hindi is qualifying (fail it and your GS scores never get read); the Essay is 150 merit
marks that toppers treat as a scoring paper. Give them a system, not leftovers.

## Paper 1 — General Hindi (100 marks, qualifying: 30%)

| Section | Marks | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Nibandh (essay) | 30 | 300–400 words; reuse the essay framework below, shorter |
| Vyakaran (grammar) | 30 | Sandhi, samas, vilom, paryayvachi, muhavare, lokoktiyan, vakya-shuddhi |
| Apathit gadyansh | 20 | Unseen passage — read question first, then passage |
| Anuvad / sankshipt lekhan | 20 | English→Hindi translation / precis (one-third length rule) |

**The weekly system (1 hour/week is enough for Hindi-background candidates):**
- 30 min: one grammar topic — write 20 examples by hand, not just reading rules.
- 20 min: one unseen passage or one precis under time.
- 10 min: revise a personal error log (the same sandhi/samas mistakes repeat — log them).

> For non-Hindi-background candidates: double the time, start in month one, and treat the
> 30-mark essay as your safety net — it is the most learnable section.

## Paper 5 — Essay / Nibandh (150 marks, merit)

Essays come in flavours: social issues, **Bihar-specific topics**, economy, governance,
science & tech, and the dreaded philosophical quote.

![Essay writing framework: six dimensions and structure](assets/svg/essay-framework.svg)

### The six-dimension lens

Run **every** topic through all six before writing: Historical → Economic → Social →
Political → Environmental → Ethical/Bihar. The lens guarantees you never run dry mid-essay,
and the Bihar dimension is your differentiator on this paper.

### Structure: 15 – 70 – 15

- **Intro (~15%):** hook — a quote, anecdote, or striking statistic; define the key term.
  Never start with "X is a very important topic…".
- **Body (~70%):** one paragraph per dimension, 4–6 subheaded sections, one data point or
  example in each. Counter-view paragraph included — one-sided essays cap your marks.
- **Conclusion (~15%):** balanced verdict, way forward, echo the intro's quote or image.

### Decoding the philosophical quote (worked example)

**Topic type:** *"Technology: servant or master?"*
- Don't philosophise in the air — decode into dimensions: history (industrial revolutions),
  economy (jobs, productivity), society (attention, relationships), ethics (AI, privacy),
  Bihar (digital divide — who is the "servant" for?).
- Thesis line: "Technology is a servant that becomes a master when governance sleeps."
  Everything in the body then proves or qualifies that line.

### Essay practice rules

1. **One full essay per week from month 4** — 1000–1200 words, timed (~90 min).
2. One Bihar example minimum per essay (Chhath and floods are overused — reach for
   Nalanda's revival, the 50% panchayat reservation, makhana's GI journey).
3. Get two essays a month reviewed by a mentor or peer group; self-review misses your
   own blind spots.

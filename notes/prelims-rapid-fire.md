# Prelims Rapid-Fire One-Liners

High-yield facts for the three heaviest Prelims blocks — Bihar Special (~24), Science (~27),
Current Affairs (~23) — plus quick hits from the other subjects. All written fresh for this
site. Read a block, cover the answers, repeat weekly.

## Bihar Special

- Ganga enters Bihar at **Chausa (Buxar)** and exits at **Katihar**; it bisects the state west→east.
- North-bank Himalayan rivers: **Gandak, Burhi Gandak, Bagmati, Kosi, Mahananda** — flood-prone.
- South-bank rivers: **Son, Punpun, Falgu** — seasonal, rain-fed.
- Kosi is called the **"Sorrow of Bihar"** for its course-shifting floods.
- Bihar has **38 districts** and **9 divisions**.
- Bihar was the **first Indian state with 50% reservation for women in panchayats**.
- Bihar has a **bicameral legislature** — Vidhan Sabha + Vidhan Parishad.
- **Patna High Court** was established in **1916**.
- Nalanda University was founded under **Kumaragupta I (Gupta)**; Vikramshila under **Dharmapala (Pala)**.
- Mahavira was born at **Vaishali**; Buddha attained enlightenment at **Bodh Gaya**.
- The first Buddhist council was held at **Rajgir**, the second at **Vaishali**.
- **Sher Shah Suri** belonged to **Sasaram (Bihar)**; built the Grand Trunk Road, introduced the rupiya.
- The Battle of **Buxar (1764)** was fought in Bihar — it gave the Company the Diwani of Bengal (1765).
- **Kunwar Singh** of Jagdishpur/Arrah led the 1857 revolt in Bihar, fighting past the age of 80.
- **Champaran Satyagraha (1917)** was Gandhi's first satyagraha in India — against the tinkathia system.
- **Dr. Rajendra Prasad**, India's first President, was from **Ziradei (Siwan), Bihar**.
- The **JP Movement (1974)** began from Bihar against corruption and misgovernance.
- **Chhath** is Bihar's defining festival, dedicated to the Sun god (Kartik month).
- **Sonepur Mela** (Saran) is among Asia's largest cattle fairs, held at the Ganga–Gandak confluence.
- **Madhubani/Mithila painting** holds a **GI tag**; traditionally painted by Maithil women.
- **Makhana** (fox nut) — Bihar is India's **#1 producer** (Mithila region); holds a GI tag.
- **Litchi** — Bihar is India's **#1 producer** (Muzaffarpur belt).
- **Saat Nischay (2.0)**: Bihar's seven-resolves governance programme.
- **Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana**: lifecycle support for the girl child (birth → graduation).
- **Bihar Student Credit Card**: education loans for higher studies.
- **Valmiki Tiger Reserve** (West Champaran) is Bihar's only tiger reserve.
- **Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary** (Bhagalpur) protects the Ganga dolphin.
- **Kanwar Lake** (Begusarai) is among Asia's largest freshwater oxbow lakes.
- The Ganga dolphin is India's **National Aquatic Animal**.

## General Science

- Vitamin C → **scurvy**; Vitamin D → **rickets**; Vitamin B1 → **beriberi**; Vitamin A → **night blindness**.
- Human blood pH ≈ **7.4** (slightly alkaline).
- **Photosynthesis**: CO₂ + water → glucose + oxygen, in chlorophyll under sunlight.
- The **ozone layer** lies in the **stratosphere**; CFCs deplete it.
- **Newton's third law** (action–reaction) explains rocket propulsion.
- **Sound travels fastest in solids**, slowest in gases; it needs a medium — silence in space.
- **Red blood cells** carry oxygen via haemoglobin; **white blood cells** fight infection; **platelets** clot blood.
- **Insulin** (pancreas) regulates blood sugar; its deficiency causes diabetes.
- **Mitochondria** = powerhouse of the cell; **ribosomes** = protein synthesis.
- **Xylem** transports water up a plant; **phloem** transports food.
- **Acids** turn blue litmus red; **bases** turn red litmus blue.
- **Diamond** and **graphite** are both pure carbon — different crystal structures.
- **Washing soda** = sodium carbonate; **baking soda** = sodium bicarbonate.
- **Rust** is hydrated iron oxide — needs both air and moisture.
- **SI unit of force**: newton; of energy: joule; of power: watt; of electric current: ampere.
- **Aditya-L1** is India's first solar observatory mission (Lagrange point L1).
- **Gaganyaan** is India's planned human spaceflight mission.

## Current Affairs (method + anchors)

- Cover the **last 12–18 months**; BPSC rewards the *static link* behind the headline.
- For every scheme: **ministry + launch year + target group + one Bihar angle**.
- Track: Union Budget highlights, Economic Survey of Bihar, Bihar state budget.
- Track: ISRO missions, national awards (Bharat Ratna, Padma), major sports events.
- Maintain a **one-page monthly sheet**; revise the full stack in the final 30 days.

## Polity quick hits

- **Article 32**: right to constitutional remedies — "heart and soul of the Constitution".
- **Article 14–18**: right to equality; **19–22**: freedoms; **25–28**: religious freedom.
- **Money Bill** (Art. 110): Lok Sabha only, Speaker certifies, Rajya Sabha gets 14 days.
- **Joint sitting** (Art. 108): only for ordinary bills, never for Money Bills or constitutional amendments.
- **73rd amendment (1992)**: Panchayati Raj; **74th**: municipalities; **101st**: GST.
- **42nd amendment**: added "Socialist, Secular, Integrity" to the Preamble.

## Economy & Geography quick hits

- GDP = **C + I + G + (X − M)** (expenditure approach).
- **Repo rate ↑** → borrowing costlier → inflation cools (RBI's main lever).
- **Fiscal deficit** = total expenditure − total receipts excluding borrowings.
- **GST** is a destination-based indirect tax (101st amendment, 2016; launched 2017).
- **MGNREGA** guarantees 100 days of wage employment per rural household per year.
- Southwest monsoon: **differential heating** → low pressure over land → moist ocean winds in.
- **Arabian Sea branch** hits the Western Ghats first; **Bay of Bengal branch** feeds Bihar.
- **Black (regur) soil** suits cotton; **alluvial soil** dominates the Bihar–UP plains.

## History quick hits

- IVC: **Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Lothal, Dholavira** — town planning, drainage.
- **322 BCE**: Chandragupta Maurya founds the Mauryan empire (capital **Pataliputra**).
- **Ashoka's** dhamma policy followed the **Kalinga war (261 BCE)**.
- **1857** → **1885** (INC) → **1917** (Champaran) → **1942** (Quit India) → **1947**.

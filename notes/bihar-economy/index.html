Bihar Economy — Original Notes

Structure

  • Agriculture-dominant economy: a large share of the workforce depends on farming and allied

activities, though services now lead GSDP.

  • Per-capita income is among the lowest in India — but **GSDP growth has frequently outpaced the

national average** in recent years ("Bihar growth story" is a mains favourite).

  • Key constraints: floods (north), low industrialization, out-migration of labour.

Agriculture

  • Cropping: rice (kharif), wheat (rabi), maize, pulses, oilseeds, sugarcane, jute (Purnia–Kishanganj

belt).

  • India #1 in litchi (Muzaffarpur; Shahi Litchi — GI tag) and India #1 in makhana/foxnut

(Madhubani–Darbhanga; GI tag).

  • Horticulture push: banana, mango (Bhagalpuri Zardalu — GI tag), vegetables.
  • Allied: dairy (Sudha/Comfed cooperatives), fisheries (ponds, wetlands), poultry.

Industry

  • Pre-2000 Bihar had mineral-based industry; after Jharkhand's separation, the mineral base

shrank sharply.

  • Existing: sugar mills, rice/flour mills, handloom & silk (Bhagalpur silk — GI tag),

food processing.

  • New thrust: ethanol plants, IT/industrial parks, Bihar Industrial Investment Promotion

framework; improved power availability as an enabler.

Infrastructure & energy

  • Road connectivity improved sharply (state highways, rural roads); bridges across the Ganga

(Gandhi Setu, new parallel bridges) are exam-worthy examples.

  • Rail: Danapur division (ECR, Hajipur HQ) — note East Central Railway HQ is at Hajipur.
  • Power: thermal plants at Barh, Nabinagar, Buxar; the state still imports much of its power.

Census 2011 highlights (Bihar)

  • Population: ~10.41 crore — 3rd most populous state in India.
  • Density: 1,106 persons/sq km — highest among major states.
  • Literacy: 61.8% — lowest among states (male ~71%, female ~51%).
  • Sex ratio: 918 females per 1000 males.
  • Urbanization among the lowest in India (~11%).

Development issues (mains answer material)

  • Floods & migration — annual north-Bihar floods displace lakhs; Bihar is India's biggest

source of migrant labour.

  • Human development — health and education indicators lag; schemes like Kanya Utthan and the

cycle yojana target enrolment and gender gaps.

  • Way forward points for answers: agro-processing value chains (litchi, makhana, mango),

flood management with Nepal cooperation, skill development for migrants, industrial investment climate.

Budget & finance basics

  • State budget presented annually; key heads: education, health, rural development, energy.
  • GST compensation era ended; states rely on own tax revenue + central devolution (Finance

Commission).

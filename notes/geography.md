# Indian Geography — Original Notes (BPSC-oriented)

## Physiography

- **Himalayas** — young fold mountains; three parallel ranges: Himadri (Greater), Himachal
  (Lesser), Shiwaliks (Outer). Highest Indian peak: **K2** (PoK) / Kanchenjunga (within India).
- **Northern Plains** — Indus–Ganga–Brahmaputra; bhabar → terai → bhangar → khadar (new alluvium).
- **Peninsular Plateau** — oldest landmass; Deccan Traps (volcanic basalt → black soil).
- Coasts: western (narrow, estuaries) vs eastern (wide, deltas); islands: Andaman–Nicobar
  (Bay of Bengal), Lakshadweep (Arabian Sea).

## Rivers

- **Himalayan:** perennial, antecedent. Indus system (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej);
  **Ganga system** (Ganga + Yamuna, Ghaghra, Gandak, Kosi, Son); Brahmaputra system.
- **Peninsular:** seasonal, consequent. East-flowing (deltas): Mahanadi, Godavari (longest
  peninsular), Krishna, Kaveri. West-flowing (estuaries): Narmada, Tapi.
- **Bihar's rivers:** Ganga (bisects the state), Gandak, Kosi ("sorrow of Bihar"), Son, Bagmati,
  Mahananda, Punpun, Falgu (Gaya).

## Climate

- **Monsoon mechanism** — differential heating of land/sea; SW monsoon (June–Sep, ~75% of rain);
  NE monsoon (Oct–Dec, Tamil Nadu coast).
- El Niño (warming, weak monsoon) vs La Niña (cooling, strong monsoon).
- Seasons: winter (Jan–Feb), summer (Mar–May), monsoon (Jun–Sep), retreating (Oct–Dec).

## Soils

- **Alluvial** (plains, most fertile) → **Black/regur** (Deccan, cotton) → **Red** (Tamil Nadu,
  Karnataka) → **Laterite** (high rainfall, leached) → Desert (arid) → Mountain/forest soils.

## Agriculture

- Kharif (monsoon: rice, maize, cotton, soybean) / Rabi (winter: wheat, barley, mustard, gram) /
  Zaid (short summer: watermelon, vegetables).
- Green Revolution (1960s, HYV seeds, Punjab–Haryana–W. UP); White (milk), Blue (fisheries),
  Yellow (oilseeds) revolutions.
- **Bihar:** rice–wheat dominant; **#1 in litchi and makhana** nationally.

## Minerals & industry

- Minerals: coal (Jharia, Raniganj), iron ore (Odisha–Jharkhand belt), bauxite (Odisha), mica.
- Industries: iron & steel (Jamshedpur, Bokaro, Bhilai, Rourkela), cotton textiles (Mumbai–
  Ahmedabad), IT (Bengaluru, Hyderabad).
- **Bihar:** agro-processing, Bhagalpur silk, food processing; post-2000 mineral base is small.

## Population (Census 2011)

- 121 crore; growth rate declining; **Bihar: 10.41 crore (3rd), density 1106 (highest among major
  states), literacy 61.8% (lowest), sex ratio 918.**
- Demographic dividend window: median age ~28.

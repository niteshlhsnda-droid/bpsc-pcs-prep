Bihar Polity — Original Notes

State legislature

  • Bicameral — Bihar is one of the few states with two houses:
    • Vidhan Sabha (Legislative Assembly): 243 seats
    • Vidhan Parishad (Legislative Council): 75 seats
  • Assembly term: 5 years. The November 2025 assembly election returned the NDA with 202 of

243 seats (BJP 89, JD(U) 85, allies the rest).

Executive (as of September 2026 — re-verify before the exam)

  • Chief Minister: Samrat Choudhary (BJP) — sworn in 15 April 2026, the **first BJP Chief

Minister of Bihar**, after Nitish Kumar resigned (ending a ~two-decade tenure) and moved to the Rajya Sabha.

  • Deputy CMs: Bijendra Prasad Yadav and Vijay Kumar Choudhary (JD(U)).
  • Governor administers the oath; the CM must command the Assembly's confidence.

Panchayati Raj (very high weightage)

  • Three-tier system: Gram Panchayat → Panchayat Samiti (block) → Zila Parishad (district).
  • Bihar was the first state to reserve 50% of PRI seats for women (Bihar Panchayat Raj Act,

2006) — a landmark, frequently asked.

  • Gram Katchahari — village-level dispute resolution body headed by the Sarpanch; handles

petty civil/criminal matters.

  • Reservations also for SC/ST/OBC/EBC in proportion to population.
  • Key functionaries: Mukhiya (Gram Panchayat head), Pramukh (Panchayat Samiti), Adhyaksha

(Zila Parishad).

Urban local bodies

  • Nagar Panchayat, Nagar Parishad, Nagar Nigam (e.g., Patna Municipal Corporation).
  • 74th Amendment framework; state election commission conducts local body polls.

Judiciary

  • Patna High Court — established 1916; one of India's oldest high courts.
  • District & sessions courts; Lok Adalats; Bihar State Legal Services Authority.

Key state schemes (Saat Nischay and beyond)

  • Saat Nischay (7 Resolves) — the state's flagship development programme:
    • Part 1 (2015–20): Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal, Ghar Tak Pakki Gali-Naliyan, toilet in every home,

electricity to every home, etc.

  • Part 2 (2020–25): youth employment/skill focus, irrigation, connectivity.
  • Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana — support for the girl child from birth through

graduation.

  • Bihar Student Credit Card — education loans for higher studies.
  • Mukhyamantri Balika Cycle Yojana — bicycles for schoolgirls (credited with raising

girls' enrolment).

  • Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal — piped drinking water to every household.
  • Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali — water conservation and afforestation mission.

Administration

  • Divisions (9) headed by Commissioners; districts (38) headed by District Magistrates.
  • Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) — constitutional body under Article 315; conducts the CCE.
  • Bihar Staff Selection Commission, Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission for other posts.

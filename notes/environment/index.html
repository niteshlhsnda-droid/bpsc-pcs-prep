Environment & Ecology — Original Notes (BPSC-oriented)

Basics

  • Ecology = study of organism–environment relations; ecosystem = biotic + abiotic interacting

system; biome = large regional ecosystem (tropical rainforest, savanna, desert...).

  • Ecological pyramids (number, biomass, energy — energy pyramid is always upright); food chains

vs food webs; 10% law (Lindeman) — only ~10% energy transfers between trophic levels.

  • Ecological succession: primary (bare rock) → secondary (disturbed area) → climax community.

Biodiversity

  • India is one of 17 megadiverse countries; ~7–8% of recorded species on ~2.4% of land area.
  • Biodiversity hotspots in/near India: Western Ghats, Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Sundaland

(Nicobar).

  • Levels: genetic, species, ecosystem diversity. Endemic = found only in one region.

Conservation framework

  • Wildlife Protection Act, 1972 — schedules I–VI; National Parks (no human activity) vs

Wildlife Sanctuaries (some permitted) vs Biosphere Reserves (core/buffer/transition zones).

  • Project Tiger (1973) — now 50+ tiger reserves; Valmiki Tiger Reserve (West Champaran)

is Bihar's only tiger reserve. All-India tiger estimation every 4 years.

  • Project Elephant (1992). Ramsar Convention (1971) — wetlands; **Kanwar Lake (Begusarai,

Bihar)** is a Ramsar site.

  • Forest Conservation Act, 1980; Environment Protection Act, 1986 (after Bhopal, 1984);

Biological Diversity Act, 2002; CAMPA; Green India Mission.

Climate change

  • Greenhouse gases: CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, water vapour, CFCs/HFCs. Enhanced greenhouse effect →

global warming.

  • Key agreements: UNFCCC (1992, Rio) → Kyoto Protocol (1997, binding on developed) →

Paris Agreement (2015) — well below 2°C, aim 1.5°C; NDCs; net-zero pledges (India: 2070).

  • IPCC reports (AR6); concepts: carbon footprint, carbon trading, blue carbon (mangroves).

Pollution

  • Air: PM2.5/PM10, smog, NCAP (National Clean Air Programme); Graded Response Action Plan

(Delhi-NCR).

  • Water: BOD/COD, eutrophication; Namami Gange programme for Ganga rejuvenation.
  • Soil/noise/plastic: Plastic Waste Management Rules; single-use plastic ban (2022);

E-waste rules.

Bihar environment angles (mains-ready)

  • Annual floods (Kosi/Gandak) as a disaster-management + climate-adaptation question.
  • Low forest cover; Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali mission (water conservation + afforestation).
  • Wetlands: Kanwar Lake; dolphins: Gangetic dolphin (national aquatic animal) in the Ganga —

Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary (Bhagalpur) is India's only dolphin sanctuary.

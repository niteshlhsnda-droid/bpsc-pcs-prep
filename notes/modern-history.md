# Modern Indian History — Original Notes (BPSC-oriented)

## British conquest

- **Plassey (1757)** — Clive vs Siraj-ud-Daulah; British gained Bengal's revenue.
- **Buxar (1764)** — fought in **Bihar**; British defeated the combined forces of Mir Qasim,
  Shuja-ud-Daula, and Shah Alam II. Decisive: gave the Company the Diwani of Bengal, Bihar,
  Orissa (1765).
- Subsidiary Alliance (Wellesley), Doctrine of Lapse (Dalhousie) — tools of expansion.

## 1857 Revolt

- Causes: political, economic, military (greased cartridges as trigger); began at Meerut
  (10 May 1857).
- Centres & leaders: Delhi (Bahadur Shah Zafar), Kanpur (Nana Saheb), Lucknow (Begum Hazrat
  Mahal), Jhansi (Rani Lakshmibai), **Arrah/Jagdishpur — Kunwar Singh (Bihar)**.
- Failed due to lack of unified leadership, limited spread, British resources — but ended
  Company rule (Government of India Act, 1858).

## Social reform

- Raja Ram Mohan Roy (Brahmo Samaj, 1828; sati abolition 1829), Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar
  (widow remarriage 1856), Dayanand Saraswati (Arya Samaj 1875), Ramakrishna Mission (1897,
  Vivekananda), Theosophical Society (1875), Aligarh Movement (Syed Ahmed Khan).

## Congress & the national movement

- INC founded **1885** (A.O. Hume); early moderates → extremists (1905–1908, Bengal partition).
- **Gandhian phase:**
  - **Champaran Satyagraha (1917)** — first satyagraha; indigo/tinkathia; Raj Kumar Shukla
  - Kheda (1918), Ahmedabad mill strike (1918)
  - Non-Cooperation (1920–22) — Jallianwala Bagh (1919) as trigger; Chauri Chaura (1922) as halt
  - Civil Disobedience (1930) — Dandi March; Gandhi–Irwin Pact (1931); Round Table Conferences
  - **Quit India (1942)** — "Do or Die"; Bihar saw intense participation
- Revolutionary strand: Bhagat Singh (HSRA, 1928; Lahore conspiracy), Chandrashekhar Azad,
  Surya Sen (Chittagong, 1930).
- Subhas Chandra Bose — Forward Bloc (1939), **INA (1943)**; "Give me blood...".
- Cripps Mission (1942), Cabinet Mission (1946), Mountbatten Plan → **Independence, 15 Aug 1947**.

## Post-independence consolidation

- Partition; integration of princely states (Sardar Patel; Hyderabad 1948, Junagadh, Kashmir).
- Constitution adopted 26 Nov 1949, in force 26 Jan 1950.
- **JP Movement (1974)** — Sampoorna Kranti from Bihar; Emergency (1975–77); Janata government
  (1977); return of Congress (1980).

## One-line anchors (memorize)

- 1757 Plassey → 1764 Buxar → 1857 Revolt → 1885 INC → 1917 Champaran → 1919 Jallianwala →
  1920 NCM → 1930 CDM → 1942 Quit India → 1947 Independence.

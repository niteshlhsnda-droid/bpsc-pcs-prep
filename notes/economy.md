# Indian Economy — Original Notes (BPSC-oriented)

## Basics

- **GDP** = value of final goods & services produced within borders in a year; **GNP** adds net
  factor income from abroad; **per-capita income** = national income ÷ population.
- Sectors: primary (agri), secondary (industry), tertiary (services — now ~55% of India's GDP).
- **Base year for GDP: 2011–12**; measured by NSO (Ministry of Statistics).

## Planning to NITI

- Five-Year Plans (1951–2017); 1st (Harrod–Domar, agriculture), 2nd (Mahalanobis, heavy industry).
- **NITI Aayog (2015)** replaced the Planning Commission — cooperative federalism think-tank.
- **1991 LPG reforms** — liberalization, privatization, globalization; Narasimha Rao government,
  Manmohan Singh as FM; forex crisis as trigger.

## Money, banking, inflation

- **RBI** (1935; nationalized 1949) — monetary policy; **repo rate** (RBI lends to banks),
  **reverse repo**, CRR, SLR.
- **Inflation** measured by **CPI** (retail) and **WPI** (wholesale); RBI targets CPI ~4% (±2%).
- Banks: SBI (largest PSB), NABARD (agri refinance), recent bank mergers/consolidations.

## Fiscal policy & tax

- **Budget** (Art 112); revenue vs capital receipts/expenditure; **fiscal deficit** =
  borrowings needed.
- **GST (101st Amendment, 2017)** — "one nation, one tax"; CGST+SGST/IGST; GST Council (Art 279A)
  chaired by Union FM.
- Direct taxes (income, corporate) vs indirect (GST, customs).

## Poverty & employment

- Poverty lines: Tendulkar Committee, Rangarajan Committee; **Multidimensional Poverty Index**
  (NITI Aayog) now the standard lens.
- **MGNREGA (2005)** — 100 days guaranteed wage employment per rural household per year.
- Unemployment types: disguised (agri), structural, cyclical; PLFS data.

## Key schemes (centre)

- PM-JAY (health insurance), PM-KISAN (income support), PM Awas Yojana (housing), Jal Jeevan
  Mission (tap water), PM Mudra Yojana (micro-enterprise loans), Stand-Up India, Digital India.

## Bihar economy linkages

- Agriculture share high; services rising; **GSDP growth often above national average** in
  recent years despite low per-capita income.
- State flagships: **Saat Nischay**, Kanya Utthan, Student Credit Card, Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal.
- Development answers: agro-processing (litchi/makhana/mango value chains), flood management,
  migration & skilling, industrial investment climate.

## Trade & external

- Current account vs capital account; **forex reserves** with RBI; rupee convertibility
  (current account full, capital account partial).
- WTO (1995); FTAs; India's services exports (IT) strength.

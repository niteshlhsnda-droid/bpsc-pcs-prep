# Bihar History — Original Notes

Bihar is the single highest-weightage history section in BPSC. Ancient Bihar *is* ancient India
for several centuries — Magadh was the centre of the subcontinent's first empires.

## Ancient Bihar

- **Magadh mahajanapada** — first among the sixteen mahajanapadas; capital moved from Rajgir
  (Rajagriha) to **Pataliputra** (modern Patna) under the Haryanka/Shishunaga dynasties.
- **Nanda dynasty** — first non-kshatriya dynasty; Mahapadma Nanda; vast empire that Alexander's
  soldiers feared to face (321 BCE context).
- **Mauryan Empire (321–185 BCE)** — Chandragupta Maurya (with Chanakya's guidance) founded it
  from Pataliputra. Ashoka's capital was Pataliputra; the **lion capital at Sarnath** and edicts
  across India date to him.
- **Buddhism & Jainism** — Buddha attained enlightenment at **Bodh Gaya**; first Buddhist council
  at **Rajgir**; second at **Vaishali**. Mahavira was born at **Vaishali** (Kundagrama).
- **Nalanda University** — founded in the 5th century CE under **Kumaragupta I** (Gupta);
  flourished under Harsha; destroyed in the 12th century during Bakhtiyar Khilji's raids.
- **Vikramshila University** — founded by **Dharmapala** of the **Pala dynasty** (8th century CE),
  near Bhagalpur; a great centre of Tantric Buddhism.
- **Pala dynasty (8th–12th century)** — ruled Bihar–Bengal; patrons of Buddhism and the
  Vikramshila/Odantapuri universities.

## Medieval Bihar

- **Sher Shah Suri (1486–1545)** — born in Sasaram, Bihar; his tomb is at **Sasaram**. Reforms:
  land revenue system (zabt/ryotwari precursor), **rupiya** currency, Grand Trunk Road
  (Sadak-e-Azam), dak/postal system.
- Bihar under the Delhi Sultanate and then the Mughals; Maner Sharif and Bihar Sharif as Sufi
  centres.

## Freedom Movement — Bihar's role

- **Champaran Satyagraha (1917)** — Gandhi's **first satyagraha in India**; against the tinkathia
  system forcing indigo cultivation. Leaders: Raj Kumar Shukla (who brought Gandhi to Champaran),
  with support from J.B. Kripalani, Rajendra Prasad, Mazharul Haque.
- **1857 Revolt** — **Kunwar Singh** (Veer Kunwar Singh) of Jagdishpur (Arrah) led the revolt in
  Bihar at age ~80; died in 1858.
- **Non-Cooperation / Civil Disobedience / Quit India** — Bihar participated strongly; the
  **Quit India movement (1942)** saw intense activity in Patna (secretariat firing) and rural Bihar.
- **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** — born in Ziradei (Siwan), Bihar; first President of India; president of
  the Constituent Assembly.
- **JP Movement (1974)** — **Jayaprakash Narayan** launched the **Sampoorna Kranti (Total
  Revolution)** movement from Bihar against corruption and misgovernance; a direct precursor to
  the Emergency (1975–77).

## Modern political milestones

- **1912** — Bihar & Orissa carved out of Bengal as a separate province (Bihar Diwas: **22 March**).
- **1936** — Bihar became a separate province (Orissa separated).
- **1946/1950** — **Sri Krishna Sinha** became Premier/CM; **Jairamdas Daulatram** first Governor.
- **2000** — Jharkhand carved out of Bihar (15 November 2000); Bihar left with 38 districts.

## Personalities to remember

- **Chanakya (Kautilya)** — Arthashastra; mentor of Chandragupta Maurya; associated with
  Pataliputra/Takshashila.
- **Ashoka** — Mauryan emperor; Dhamma policy after Kalinga.
- **Aryabhata** — astronomer-mathematician; associated with Pataliputra (Aryabhatiya, place-value
  system, approximation of pi).
- **Sher Shah Suri** — Sasaram; administration that the Mughals later copied.
- **Kunwar Singh** — 1857, Jagdishpur.
- **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** — first President; born Ziradei, Siwan.
- **Jayaprakash Narayan** — Sampoorna Kranti, 1974.
- **Ramdhari Singh Dinkar** — nationalist Hindi poet (Rashmirathi, Kurukshetra); born in Simaria,
  Begusarai.
- **Bhikhari Thakur** — "Shakespeare of Bhojpuri"; creator of the **Bidesia** folk theatre form.

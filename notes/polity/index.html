Indian Polity — Original Notes (BPSC-oriented)

The Constitution

  • Drafted by the Constituent Assembly (president: Dr. Rajendra Prasad — born in Bihar;

chairman of Drafting Committee: B.R. Ambedkar).

  • Adopted 26 Nov 1949; commenced 26 Jan 1950. World's longest written constitution.
  • Preamble — Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity; "Socialist, Secular" added by the

42nd Amendment (1976). Preamble is part of the Constitution (Kesavananda Bharati, 1973).

Fundamental Rights (Part III, Articles 12–35)

  • Art 14 — equality before law; Art 15 — no discrimination; Art 16 — equality of

opportunity in public employment.

  • Art 19 — six freedoms (speech, assembly, association, movement, residence, profession).
  • Art 21 — life and personal liberty (expanded by courts: privacy, dignity, clean

environment).

  • Art 21A — free & compulsory education (6–14 years; 86th Amendment, 2002).
  • Art 25–28 — freedom of religion. Art 29–30 — cultural & educational rights of minorities.
  • Art 32 — right to constitutional remedies ("heart and soul" — Ambedkar); writs:

habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, certiorari, quo warranto.

DPSP & Fundamental Duties

  • DPSP (Part IV, Art 36–51) — non-justiciable directives; Ireland-inspired.
  • Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Art 51A) — 11 duties; added by 42nd Amendment (originally 10).

Union & State

  • President (Art 52–62): elected by electoral college; impeachment (Art 61).
  • PM & Council of Ministers: Art 74 (aid & advise), Art 75 (collective responsibility to

Lok Sabha).

  • Parliament: Lok Sabha (max 550; current 543 elected) + Rajya Sabha (max 250; current 245).
  • Governor (Art 153–167): appointed by President; "pleasure" doctrine; key role in hung

assemblies.

  • Bihar: bicameral — Assembly 243, Council 75.

Judiciary

  • Supreme Court (Art 124–147); High Courts (Art 214–231) — Patna HC established 1916.
  • Judicial review; basic structure doctrine (Kesavananda Bharati, 1973).
  • Recent: collegium system, NJAC struck down (2015).

Federalism & amendments

  • 7th Schedule: Union, State, Concurrent lists.
  • Key amendments: 42nd (1976) — "mini-constitution"; 44th (1978) — right to property moved

to Art 300A; 52nd (1985) — anti-defection (10th Schedule); 73rd/74th (1992) — PRIs/ULBs; 86th (2002) — RTE; 101st (2016) — GST; 103rd (2019) — EWS reservation; 106th (2023) — women's reservation (Nari Shakti Vandan).

Bodies

  • Election Commission (Art 324); CAG (Art 148); UPSC/SPSC (Art 315–323); Finance Commission

(Art 280); NITI Aayog (2015, replaced Planning Commission).

Science & Technology — Original Notes (BPSC-oriented)

Study NCERT-level science (Class 6–10) for concepts + current missions for the "tech" part. BPSC's science section is mostly concept + current affairs, not deep theory.

Space (highest weightage)

  • ISRO (1969; HQ Bengaluru; under Department of Space).
  • Launch vehicles: PSLV (workhorse, polar orbits), GSLV (cryogenic upper stage),

LVM3 (heavy lift — Chandrayaan-2/3, Gaganyaan).

  • Chandrayaan-3 (2023) — first soft landing near the lunar south pole; lander Vikram,

rover Pragyan.

  • Aditya-L1 (2023) — India's first solar observatory, at Lagrange point L1.
  • Gaganyaan — India's human spaceflight programme (in advanced testing as of 2026).
  • Other missions to know: Mangalyaan (2013 — first-attempt Mars success), Astrosat,

NavIC (Indian regional navigation system), NISAR (NASA–ISRO radar satellite).

Defence

  • DRDO (1958); missile families: Agni (long-range ballistic), Prithvi (short-range),

Akash (surface-to-air), BrahMos (supersonic cruise, India–Russia), Nag (anti-tank).

  • Indigenous platforms: Tejas (LCA fighter), INS Vikrant (indigenous aircraft carrier),

Arihant (nuclear submarine), Pinaka (multi-barrel rocket launcher).

  • Tri-services: Army/Navy/Air Force + CDS (Chief of Defence Staff) structure.

IT & digital

  • Digital India pillars: digital infrastructure, governance on demand, digital empowerment.
  • Emerging tech: AI (IndiaAI Mission), 5G rollout, semiconductors (India Semiconductor

Mission), quantum (National Quantum Mission), cybersecurity (CERT-In).

  • UPI — India's digital payments stack; ONDC for e-commerce; DigiLocker.

Biotechnology & health

  • Vaccines: India's COVID programme (Covishield/Covaxin) — largest vaccination drive context.
  • Biotech: Bt cotton (only commercialized GM crop in India), GM mustard (Dhara Mustard

Hybrid-11 — regulatory debate), genome sequencing (Genome India project).

  • Health schemes: PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat) — world's largest health assurance scheme;

Mission Indradhanush (immunization).

Energy

  • Nuclear: DAE/BARC; three-stage programme (Homi Bhabha); Kudankulam, Kakrapar plants.
  • Renewables: International Solar Alliance (India–France, HQ Gurugram); National Green

Hydrogen Mission; wind (Tamil Nadu, Gujarat leaders); target 500 GW non-fossil by 2030.

NCERT concept anchors (prelims traps)

  • Motion, light (reflection/refraction), electricity basics; acids/bases/salts, metals &

non-metals, periodic table trends; cell structure, photosynthesis, human body systems, vitamins & deficiency diseases.

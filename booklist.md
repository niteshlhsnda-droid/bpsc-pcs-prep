# Booklist — One Shelf, No More

The rule: **one standard book per subject, finished twice, beats five books skimmed once.**
Everything below is a widely-used standard text. Editions change — buy the latest.

## Foundation (do these first)

| Subject | Book | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| All subjects | **NCERTs, Class 6–12** (History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science) | The base layer BPSC builds on; non-negotiable |
| Current affairs | A daily newspaper (Hindi or English) + monthly CA compilation | BPSC's CA is 12–18 months deep |

## Subject standards

| Subject | Book | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| Indian Polity | **M. Laxmikanth — Indian Polity** | The bible; covers every Article-based question BPSC asks |
| Modern History | **Spectrum — A Brief History of Modern India** | Crisp, exam-oriented, maps & tables included |
| Physical Geography | **G.C. Leong — Certificate Physical & Human Geography** | Concepts + diagrams; pairs with map work |
| Indian Geography | NCERT Class 11–12 + Majid Husain (optional depth) | NCERT is enough for most questions |
| Indian Economy | **Ramesh Singh — Indian Economy** | Clear on concepts BPSC repeats (GDP, inflation, GST) |
| Environment | **Shankar IAS — Environment** | One-stop for ecology, laws, conventions |
| Ancient/Medieval History | NCERT (RS Sharma / Satish Chandra themes) + Spectrum's culture section | BPSC's ancient questions are NCERT-depth |
| Science | NCERT Class 6–10 Science | Concepts only; tech comes from current affairs |

## Bihar-specific

- Any **reputed Bihar GK compilation** from an established publisher for rapid-fire facts
  (districts, schemes, firsts) — use it as a *factbook*, and verify current-affairs items
  (CM, schemes, awards) against the news, since books go stale fast.
- This repo's [`notes/`](notes/) and [`bihar-gk-rapid-fire.md`](bihar-gk-rapid-fire.md)
  are your living Bihar notes — update them as you learn.

## Mains & answer writing

- For GS-II Bihar Special: state government publications, the **Bihar Economic Survey**, and
  budget documents (all free on state government sites) — primary sources beat guidebooks.
- Essay: a quote bank + data bank you build yourself from newspapers; no book needed.

## What NOT to buy

- Multiple books for the same subject ("just in case").
- "BPSC-special" guidebooks that promise leaked patterns — the syllabus is public and free.
- More than one monthly current-affairs magazine; pick one and actually finish it.

## Reading order for a beginner

NCERTs (all) → Laxmikanth → Spectrum → Leong → Ramesh Singh → Shankar IAS → Bihar factbook →
PYQs alongside everything from month 3.

# Watch & Learn — BPSC Video Channels

Video is a supplement, not the syllabus. Use these channels the way toppers do:
**current affairs + topper interviews + quick concept revision** — then close the
app and read your notes. An hour of passive watching is worth less than twenty
minutes of active recall.

## Bihar-first channels

- **[KGS BPSC (Khan Global Studies)](https://www.youtube.com/@kgsbpsc1)** — Khan Sir's
  dedicated BPSC channel: exam overviews, strategy and Bihar-focused batches, mostly in
  Hindi. Best for: understanding *what the exam actually demands* before you plan.
- **[Khan GS Research Centre](https://www.youtube.com/@khangsresearchcentre1685)** —
  Khan Sir's main channel: GS concepts explained in famously simple Hindi. Best for:
  polity, history and economy foundations when a textbook paragraph won't click.
- **[EDU TERIA](https://www.youtube.com/@eduteria)** — Bihar-exam focused teaching,
  Hindi-friendly. Best for: subject-wise BPSC preparation and practice MCQs.
- **[Drishti IAS](https://www.youtube.com/@DrishtiIASvideos)** — daily news analysis,
  simplified concept videos and topper interviews. Best for: current affairs habit and
  seeing how toppers actually studied.

## How to use video without drowning in it

1. **Fix two slots only** — e.g. morning current-affairs video + one weekly topper
   interview. Everything else is reading and writing.
2. **Watch at 1.25x–1.5x** and pause to *recall aloud* before the teacher reveals the
   answer. Passive watching builds zero memory.
3. **Convert, don't collect** — every video must end as 5 lines in your notebook, or it
   didn't happen. See the [memorization system](strategy/memorization.md).
4. **Syllabus first** — if a video's topic isn't in the [syllabus](syllabus/), skip it.

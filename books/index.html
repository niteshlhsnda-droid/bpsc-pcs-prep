Books & Question Banks — One Shelf, No More

The rule: one standard book per subject, finished twice, beats five books skimmed once. Everything below is a widely-used standard text that actually exists — title, author and publisher verified. Editions change — buy the latest.

Copyright note: this page describes and links books; it never reproduces their contents. Buy or borrow the books themselves — do not upload scans here.

Where to buy

Buy from the publisher's own store or any major bookstore — search the exact title. Publisher stores worth checking: Disha Publication's BPSC collection and the PW Store. For official papers, always prefer https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in first.

What NOT to buy

  • Multiple books for the same subject ("just in case").
  • "BPSC-special" guidebooks that promise leaked patterns — the syllabus is public and free.
  • More than one monthly current-affairs magazine; pick one and actually finish it.
  • More than one PYQ compilation or practice-set book — one of each, solved twice.

Reading order for a beginner

NCERTs (all) → Laxmikanth → Spectrum → Leong → Ramesh Singh → Shankar IAS → Bihar factbook → PYQ compilation alongside everything from month 3 → practice sets in the last 90 days.

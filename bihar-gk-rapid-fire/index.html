Bihar GK Rapid Fire — High-Yield One-Liners

Drill these until they're reflex. Every fact here is verified from public records as of September 2026. Anything political/current re-verify before the exam.

Formation & administration

  • Bihar & Orissa were carved out of Bengal in 1912 — Bihar Diwas: 22 March.
  • Bihar became a separate province in 1936 (Orissa separated).
  • Jharkhand was carved out of Bihar on 15 November 2000.
  • 38 districts, 9 divisions; capital Patna (ancient Pataliputra).
  • Vidhan Sabha: 243 seats; Vidhan Parishad: 75 seats (bicameral legislature).
  • Patna High Court established 1916.
  • First Chief Minister: Sri Krishna Sinha; first Governor: Jairamdas Daulatram.
  • East Central Railway HQ: Hajipur.

Current executive (Sep 2026 — re-verify!)

  • CM: Samrat Choudhary (BJP) — sworn in 15 April 2026; first BJP Chief Minister of Bihar.
  • Deputy CMs: Bijendra Prasad Yadav and Vijay Kumar Choudhary.
  • Previous: Nitish Kumar resigned April 2026 after ~two decades as CM; elected to the Rajya Sabha.
  • Nov 2025 assembly election: NDA won 202 of 243 seats (BJP 89, JD(U) 85).

Rivers

  • Ganga bisects Bihar east–west; enters at Chausa (Buxar).
  • Kosi — "Sorrow of Bihar" (shifting course, floods).
  • Gandak (from Nepal), Son (from Amarkantak), Bagmati, Mahananda,

Punpun, Falgu (flows past Gaya; sacred for Pitrapaksha).

  • Sonepur Mela sits at the Ganga–Gandak confluence.

Ancient & medieval firsts

  • Nalanda University — 5th century CE, founded under Kumaragupta I (Gupta).
  • Vikramshila University — 8th century CE, founded by Dharmapala (Pala), near Bhagalpur.
  • Buddha's enlightenment: Bodh Gaya; first Buddhist council: Rajgir; second: Vaishali.
  • Mahavira born at Vaishali (Kundagrama).
  • Mauryan capital: Pataliputra; Ashoka ruled from here.
  • Sher Shah Suri — born in Sasaram; tomb at Sasaram; introduced the rupiya and the

Grand Trunk Road.

Freedom movement

  • Champaran Satyagraha (1917) — Gandhi's first satyagraha in India; against tinkathia

(indigo); Raj Kumar Shukla brought Gandhi to Champaran.

  • 1857: Kunwar Singh of Jagdishpur (Arrah) led Bihar's revolt at ~80 years of age.
  • JP Movement (1974): Jayaprakash Narayan's Sampoorna Kranti launched from Bihar.
  • Dr. Rajendra Prasad — first President of India; born Ziradei (Siwan); president of the

Constituent Assembly.

  • Buxar (1764) — decisive battle fought in Bihar; gave the Company the Diwani.

GI tags of Bihar

  • Agriculture: Shahi Litchi (Muzaffarpur), Bhagalpuri Zardalu mango, Katarni rice,

Magahi Paan, Makhana (foxnut), Maricha rice.

  • Handicraft: Madhubani paintings, Applique (Khatwa) work, Sujini embroidery,

Sikki grass products, Bhagalpur silk.

  • Food: Silao Khaja (Nalanda; GI tag December 2018).
  • Bihar leads India in GI authorised-user registrations (over 2,000).

State symbols

  • Bird: House Sparrow · Animal: Gaur · Flower: Kachnar · Tree: Peepal ·

Fruit: Mango.

Culture

  • Folk dances: Jat-Jatin, Jhijhiya, Sama Chakeva, Domkach.
  • Bidesia — Bhojpuri folk theatre created by Bhikhari Thakur ("Shakespeare of Bhojpuri").
  • Madhubani/Mithila painting — GI-tagged; traditionally by Maithil women.
  • Festivals: Chhath (biggest), Sonepur Mela, Pitrapaksha Mela (Gaya), Shravani Mela.
  • Poet: Ramdhari Singh Dinkar (Rashmirathi) — born Simaria, Begusarai.

Economy & census (2011)

  • Population 10.41 crore — 3rd most populous state.
  • Density 1,106/sq km — highest among major states.
  • Literacy 61.8% — lowest among states. Sex ratio 918.
  • India #1 in litchi and India #1 in makhana.
  • Valmiki Tiger Reserve (West Champaran) — Bihar's only tiger reserve.
  • Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary (Bhagalpur) — India's only dolphin sanctuary;

Gangetic dolphin is the national aquatic animal.

Panchayati Raj

  • Bihar first in India to give 50% reservation to women in Panchayati Raj (2006).
  • Three tiers: Gram Panchayat → Panchayat Samiti → Zila Parishad; Gram Katchahari for

village-level justice.

Flagship schemes

  • Saat Nischay (7 resolves) — Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal, Ghar Tak Pakki Gali-Naliyan, etc.
  • Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana; Student Credit Card; Balika Cycle Yojana;

Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali mission.

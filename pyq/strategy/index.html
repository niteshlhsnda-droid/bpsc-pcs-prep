PYQ Strategy — Previous Year Questions

Previous-year questions are the single most honest syllabus BPSC has ever published. The paper is the syllabus, interpreted.

Where to get official papers (link, don't photocopy)

  • Official BPSC website: https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in — the commission publishes question

papers and answer keys here. Always prefer the official source.

  • Do not upload scanned copies of BPSC papers or any publisher's solved-paper book to this

repo — those are copyrighted. Link to them instead.

The 10-year rule

Solve and analyze the last 10 years of Prelims papers (roughly 1,500 questions) before you consider your syllabus "done". For Mains, analyze the last 5 years of GS papers + essays.

Theme analysis — how to mine PYQs

  1. Tag every question by subject → topic → sub-topic in a spreadsheet (e.g.,

History > Modern > Gandhian movements > Champaran).

  1. Count frequencies. You will find BPSC repeats themes, not questions:
    • Bihar history (Nalanda, Vikramshila, Sher Shah, Champaran) — every single year.
    • Bihar rivers and floods — every single year.
    • Articles of the Constitution (14, 19, 21, 32, 368...) — every single year.
    • Census data of Bihar — almost every year.
    • GI tags, folk dances, festivals of Bihar — rising trend.
  2. Build a "repeat theme" list of your top 50 themes. These get revised weekly, forever.
  3. Study the options, not just the answer. BPSC's wrong options are next year's right

options — each option is a fact worth one line in your notes.

Prelims-specific PYQ drills

  • Elimination practice: for every mock question you miss, write why each wrong option is

wrong in one line. This converts one question into four facts.

  • Cut-off study: BPSC prelims cut-offs move with paper difficulty. Never target "just

qualifying" — target 15+ marks above last year's cut-off for your category.

  • ⅓ negative marking math: in mocks, track your "blind guess" vs "educated guess" accuracy

separately. Attempt a question only when your educated-guess accuracy on that topic exceeds 50%.

Mains-specific PYQ drills

  • Collect the last 5 years of essay topics — sort them into buckets (social, Bihar-specific,

economy, governance, tech, philosophical). Your weekly essay practice should rotate buckets.

  • For GS papers, convert repeated questions into 15-mark model answers in your own words and

keep them in a personal answer bank.

  • Notice how often Bihar examples are demanded implicitly — a GS-II answer without a Bihar

angle scores less, even when it's technically correct.

The one-attempt PYQ calendar

PhasePYQ work
Months 1–3Solve 1 prelims paper/week, untimed, open-book — learn the language of the paper
Months 4–81 prelims paper/week timed + full theme tagging; start mains answer bank
Months 9–102 papers/week; error log review before each new paper
Months 11–12Full mocks only; PYQs become warm-up sets, not study material

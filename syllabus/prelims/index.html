BPSC Prelims Syllabus (CCE)

Pattern (71st CCE): one paper, General Studies — 150 objective questions, 150 marks, 2 hours. ⅓ mark deducted per wrong answer. Qualifying only — marks don't count in the final merit, but you must clear the cut-off to reach Mains. Questions are set in Hindi and English.

Topic-wise breakdown compiled from the official syllabus; question-weightage hints are based on recent papers, not official figures. Treat them as guidance, not gospel.

1. History of India & Bihar

Ancient India

  • Indus Valley Civilization: town planning, major sites (Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Lothal, Dholavira)
  • Vedic period; Videha/Mithila connection to Bihar
  • Jainism & Buddhism: Mahavira (Vaishali), Buddha — enlightenment at Bodh Gaya, first sermon at Sarnath; councils at Rajgir and Vaishali
  • Mauryan dynasty: Chandragupta, Chanakya, Ashoka — capital Pataliputra
  • Gupta dynasty: "Golden Age", Nalanda University founded under Kumaragupta I
  • Post-Gupta & Pala dynasty: Vikramshila University founded by Dharmapala (Pala)

Medieval India

  • Delhi Sultanate: Slave, Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi dynasties
  • Mughal Empire: Babur to Aurangzeb — administration, culture
  • Bhakti & Sufi movements
  • Sher Shah Suri — Sasaram (Bihar) connection; land revenue reforms, Grand Trunk Road, rupiya

Modern India

  • British East India Company: Plassey (1757), Buxar (1764 — fought in Bihar)
  • Revolt of 1857: Kunwar Singh of Jagdishpur/Arrah
  • Social reform movements; Indian National Congress — all phases
  • Freedom struggle: Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Quit India
  • Bihar in the national movement: Champaran Satyagraha (1917), Bihar in Quit India (1942), JP Movement (1974)

2. Geography of India & Bihar

Indian Geography

  • Physical: Himalayan system, northern plains, peninsular plateau, coasts, islands
  • Climate & monsoon mechanism; soils; natural vegetation
  • Rivers: Himalayan (Ganga, Brahmaputra) vs peninsular systems
  • Economic geography: agriculture, industries, minerals, energy
  • Transport & communication; population & Census

Bihar Geography (high weightage)

  • Physical features: North Bihar Plain vs South Bihar Plain
  • Rivers: Ganga, Kosi, Son, Gandak, Bagmati, Mahananda, Punpun, Falgu
  • 38 districts, 9 divisions
  • Agriculture: rice, wheat; litchi — India #1; makhana — India #1
  • Floods & disaster management (Kosi — "sorrow of Bihar")
  • Minerals & industries

3. Indian Polity & Governance

  • Constitution-making; Preamble
  • Fundamental Rights (Articles 14–32); DPSP; Fundamental Duties
  • Union Government: President, PM, Parliament; State Government: Governor, CM
  • Bihar has a bicameral legislature — Vidhan Sabha + Vidhan Parishad
  • Judiciary: Supreme Court, Patna High Court
  • Centre–State relations; key amendments (42nd, 44th, 73rd, 74th, 101st)
  • Panchayati Raj — Bihar's system, 50% reservation for women (Bihar was the first state to do this)
  • Constitutional bodies: EC, CAG, UPSC; governance: e-governance, RTI, Lokpal

4. Indian & Bihar Economy

  • Planning era & NITI Aayog; 1991 LPG reforms
  • Agriculture & food security; industry (Make in India, MSME, PLI)
  • Banking & RBI; fiscal policy — Budget, GST
  • Poverty & employment — MGNREGA
  • Bihar economy: GSDP trends, per-capita income, agriculture-dominant structure
  • State schemes: Saat Nischay (7 resolves), Student Credit Card, Kanya Utthan

5. General Science & Technology

  • NCERT-level Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • Space: ISRO — Chandrayaan, Gaganyaan, Aditya-L1
  • Defence technology; IT & digital — AI, cybersecurity
  • Health, environment basics: climate change, biodiversity, pollution

6. Current Affairs — National + Bihar

  • National policies & programmes; Bihar state schemes & development news
  • Awards & sports; science missions & discoveries
  • Bihar culture & festivals; appointments; important days

Prelims tactics (pattern-specific)

  1. 150 questions in 120 minutes = ~48 seconds per question. Practice with a timer from day one.
  2. ⅓ negative marking means: attempt only if ≥50% sure; 3 wrong answers wipe out 1 right one. Blind guessing is a losing strategy; educated guessing is a skill — learn it via mocks.
  3. Bihar portion is 20–25% of the paper — it is the highest-ROI section. Master notes/bihar-*.md + the rapid-fire file first.
  4. Current affairs of the last 12–18 months matter most; maintain a monthly one-page CA sheet.

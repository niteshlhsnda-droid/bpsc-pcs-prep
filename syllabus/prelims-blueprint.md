# Prelims Topic-wise Blueprint

**The deal:** 150 MCQs, 150 marks, 2 hours, ⅓ negative marking. Prelims is qualifying only —
but miss the cut-off and your Mains preparation never gets read. This page converts the
[10-year PYQ analysis](../pyq-analysis/) into a study plan: what to study, in what order, and
the must-know facts per subject — all written fresh for this site.

## Decade weightage → study priority

Based on the average question count per subject across the last ten BPSC papers (150 marks):

| Subject | Decade avg (of 150) | Priority | First resource |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Science | ~27 | ★★★ | NCERT 6–10 science + `notes/science-tech/` |
| Bihar Special | ~24 | ★★★ | `notes/bihar-*` + [Bihar GK rapid-fire](../bihar-gk-rapid-fire/) |
| Current Affairs | ~23 | ★★★ | 12–18 month monthly sheets |
| Modern History | ~16 | ★★★ | `notes/modern-history/` |
| Geography | ~13 | ★★ | `notes/geography/` + `notes/bihar-geography/` |
| Polity | ~11 | ★★ | `notes/polity/` + `notes/bihar-polity/` |
| Economy | ~11 | ★★ | `notes/economy/` + `notes/bihar-economy/` |
| Mental Ability | 10 (fixed) | ★★ | Daily 20-question drill |
| Ancient + Medieval History | ~9 | ★★ | Timelines + map points |
| Environment | ~7–8 | ★ | `notes/environment/` |
| Art & Culture | ~4 | ★ | Festivals, GI tags, dances of Bihar |

> Roughly **90 of 150 marks** come from Science + Bihar + Current Affairs + Modern History.
> If you are short on time, that quartet is your cut-off insurance.

## 1. General Science (~27) — the quiet topper

NCERT-level Physics, Chemistry, Biology — no derivations, no numericals-heavy physics.
BPSC repeats the same *themes*: human body systems, vitamins/deficiency diseases,
motion & energy basics, acids-bases-salts, metals & reactivity, photosynthesis, ecosystems.

**Must-know (original one-liners to anchor revision):**
- Vitamin C deficiency → scurvy; D → rickets; B1 → beriberi; A → night blindness.
- pH < 7 acidic, > 7 basic; human blood ≈ 7.4 (slightly alkaline).
- Photosynthesis: CO₂ + water → glucose + O₂, powered by chlorophyll in sunlight.
- Newton's third law is the "action–reaction" pair — BPSC loves pairing it with rocket propulsion.
- Ozone layer sits in the stratosphere; CFCs deplete it.

**Mains link:** science answers in GS-II need one current-affairs tech hook (ISRO mission, AI, biotech)
per static fact — start collecting hooks from month one.

## 2. Bihar Special (~24) — highest ROI on the paper

Every year, one in six questions is about Bihar. The [Bihar notes](../notes/) plus the
[rapid-fire file](../bihar-gk-rapid-fire/) cover ~80% of this. The rest comes from state current affairs.

**Must-know clusters:**
- **Rivers:** Ganga bisects the state west→east; north-bank Himalayan rivers (Gandak, Burhi Gandak,
  Bagmati, Kosi, Mahananda) flood; south-bank rivers (Son, Punpun, Falgu) are seasonal.
- **Firsts:** first state with 50% women's reservation in panchayats; Nalanda — ancient world's
  residential university (Kumaragupta I); Vikramshila — Dharmapala (Pala).
- **Economy:** litchi and makhana — India #1 producer; Saat Nischay (2.0), Student Credit Card,
  Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana.
- **Culture:** Chhath (Kartik), Sonepur Mela (Asia's large cattle fair), Madhubani/Mithila painting
  (GI tag), Bidesia/Jhijhiya/Sohar folk forms.
- **Freedom:** Champaran Satyagraha 1917 (Gandhi's first in India), Kunwar Singh 1857, JP Movement 1974.

## 3. Current Affairs (~23) — the last 12–18 months

National schemes and policies, Bihar government schemes and appointments, awards, sports,
science missions, important days, books. Maintain a **one-page monthly sheet**; revise the stack
in the final 30 days.

**Method that works:** for every news item, ask three questions — *what happened, why it matters,
which static syllabus topic it attaches to*. Prelims tests the attachment, not the headline.

## 4. Modern History (~16) — the timeline subject

British rule → 1857 → Congress phases → Gandhian movements → independence → post-1947.
BPSC's Bihar lens: Plassey 1757 → **Buxar 1764 (fought in Bihar)** → Diwani 1765;
**Kunwar Singh** (1857); **Champaran 1917**; Bihar in Quit India 1942; JP 1974.

**Must-know anchors:** 1885 (INC founded), 1905 (Partition of Bengal), 1919 (Jallianwala +
Rowlatt), 1920–22 (Non-Cooperation), 1930 (Civil Disobedience/Dandi), 1942 (Quit India),
1947 (Independence). Learn them as a chain — each event *causes* the next.

## 5. Geography (~13) — map-first

Physical geography (relief, drainage, climate), Indian geography, then Bihar geography.
**Draw while you study** — river systems, soil belts, crop zones. One hand-drawn map is worth
ten re-read pages.

**Must-know:** southwest monsoon = differential heating (land heats faster → low pressure →
moist ocean winds in); two branches (Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal); Bihar gets the Bay branch
up the Ganga plain; Kosi = "Sorrow of Bihar".

## 6. Polity (~11) — articles over adjectives

Preamble, Fundamental Rights (14–32), DPSP, President/Parliament, Governor/CM,
Supreme Court & **Patna High Court**, 42nd/44th/73rd/74th/101st amendments,
**Panchayati Raj** (Bihar: 50% women reservation — first in India).

**Must-know:** Article 32 (constitutional remedies — "heart and soul"), Article 370's
abrogation context, Money Bill = Lok Sabha only + Speaker certifies (Art. 110),
joint sitting only for ordinary bills (Art. 108).

## 7. Economy (~11) — concepts, then Bihar

GDP approaches, inflation types, RBI & monetary policy (repo rate ↑ = inflation ↓),
fiscal deficit, GST (101st amendment), poverty & employment (MGNREGA).
Then **Bihar economy**: GSDP trends, agriculture-heavy structure, state budget highlights,
Economic Survey of Bihar.

## 8. Mental Ability (10, fixed) — free marks

Number series, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense, basic arithmetic,
data interpretation. **Never leave these** — they are the most predictable 10 marks on
the paper. 20 questions a day, timed.

## 9. Ancient + Medieval (~9) & Art & Culture (~4)

IVC town planning, Vedic literature, Mauryas (Pataliputra), Guptas (Nalanda), Palas
(Vikramshila); Sultanate → Mughals → **Sher Shah (Sasaram)** → Bhakti–Sufi.
Culture: concentrate on **Bihar** — Chhath, Madhubani, Sonepur, GI tags, folk dances.

## The 48-second discipline

150 questions ÷ 120 minutes ≈ **48 seconds each**. Build the habit early:
mock with a timer, mark-and-move on tough ones, and obey the negative-marking rule —
attempt only when at least half-sure; three wrong answers erase one right one.
Blind guessing loses; *educated* guessing (eliminate two options first) wins.

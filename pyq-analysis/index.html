🔄 The 69th CCE pattern break — what changed

Until the 68th, BPSC followed a predictable template (roughly 30 History / 30 Science / 30 Bihar / 30 Current Affairs, per Perfection IAS's 70th strategy analysis). The 69th (2023) broke it: statement-based questions appeared, General Science and Bihar sections shrank to ~20, and Polity (~13) and Economy (~14–15) suddenly mattered — sections earlier papers had nearly ignored. The 70th and 71st kept the new balance. Lesson: prepare Polity and Economy to the same depth as History now.

⚖️ Prelims vs Mains — where each topic pays off

Prelims is screening only (150 marks, ⅓ negative). The merit game is Mains (1050) + Interview (120) = 1170. Mains structure below follows the 71st CCE pattern in this repo's syllabus notes.

📝 Prelims (150 · screening)

  • General Science ~27
  • Bihar Special ~24
  • Current Affairs ~23
  • Modern History ~16
  • Geography ~13
  • Polity · Economy ~11 each
  • Mental Ability 10 fixed

✍️ Mains (1050 · merit)

  • GS-I History (incl. Bihar history) ~25–35%
  • GS-I Geography (incl. Bihar) ~20–25%
  • GS-I Polity & Governance ~20–25%
  • GS-II Economy (incl. Bihar) ~25–30%
  • GS-II Bihar Special ~30–40%
  • GS-II Science-Tech + Environment ~25–30%
  • Essay (Bihar topics frequent) 150 marks
The Mains multiplier: Bihar-focused preparation pays twice — ~24 questions in Prelims and the single biggest Mains block (GS-II's 30–40% Bihar Special). Drishti IAS's 70th Mains practice sets confirm the pattern: Bihar physiography, Bihar agriculture/industry, 1857 & Champaran in Bihar, Mauryan/Pala art and Patna Kalam recur as 38-mark questions.

🎯 Study-priority guide — what to finish first

Derived from the weightage above, not from opinion. Ordered by marks per hour of effort.

  1. Phase 1 — The Big 4 (≈100+ marks). Modern Indian History (freedom struggle), General Science (NCERT-level Physics/Chemistry/Biology), Bihar Special GK, and last-1-year Current Affairs. Physics Wallah's BPSC faculty builds its entire 100+ prelims strategy on these four — the decade data backs it: they average ~90–110 combined every year.
  2. Phase 2 — Polity + Economy (≈22–28 marks, rising). Constitution articles (14, 19, 21, 32, 368…), Panchayati Raj, and basic macro-economics + Bihar budget/economic survey. Post-69th these are no longer optional.
  3. Phase 3 — Geography (≈13 marks). Physical + Indian geography with a Bihar lens: rivers, floods, soils, physiographic divisions. Map work doubles as Mains GS-I prep.
  4. Phase 4 — Ancient/Medieval + Art & Culture (≈12 marks). Mauryan/Gupta administration, Sultanate–Mughal essentials, Bhakti–Sufi, and Bihar's art heritage (Madhubani, Patna Kalam, Pala art).
  5. Daily non-negotiable — Mental Ability (10 fixed marks). 15 minutes a day; the cheapest 10 marks in the paper.
  6. Mains overlay from day one. For every Bihar fact you learn for Prelims, write one 150-word note — it becomes a GS-II answer. Collect 5 years of essay topics and rotate buckets weekly (see PYQ strategy).

🔬 How this analysis was built — agreements & disagreements

No single coaching site publishes a perfect decade table, so figures here are normalized across sources. Here's where they converge and where they don't.

✅ Where all sources agree

  • Four blocks dominate: General Science, Bihar Special, Current Affairs and Modern History ≈ 100–110 of 150 (Physics Wallah, Testbook, CollegeDekho, theIAShub, Oliveboard).
  • Science is the biggest block at 25–30 questions since the 66th CCE — Physics, Chemistry and Biology split it roughly evenly (~8 each, Testbook).
  • 10 aptitude questions, every year, without exception.
  • Bihar never drops below ~20 — the most reliable block in the paper.
  • The 69th broke the old template — Polity and Economy rose from ~6–8 to ~13–15 (Testbook numbers; Perfection IAS analysis).

⚠️ Where sources differ (read carefully)

  • Category schemes differ: Testbook splits Bihar into 5 sub-categories; CollegeDekho groups it as one block; some sites fold schemes/indices/defence into Current Affairs while Testbook's 71st analysis lists them separately. Totals are comparable; labels aren't — we normalized to 7 blocks for the trend view.
  • Current Affairs counts vary (21–27 in Testbook's detailed table) because "CA" boundaries differ by analyst. Treat 21–27 as the honest range.
  • 71st figures are post-exam estimates from candidate feedback, shown as ranges — the official BPSC answer key is the final authority.
  • Plutus IAS and Eduteria (recommended by the user) publish courses and Bihar current-affairs material rather than public topic-weightage tables — no numeric analysis of theirs is used here; they're credited as preparation resources.
